The experience behind PostOpz

The system came from the work.

PostOpz was not built from generic documentation or abstract theory. It came from years inside real post pipelines—organizing media, building projects, supporting editors, solving workflow problems, managing handoffs, and protecting creative time across documentary and unscripted productions.

Across assistant editing, picture editing, and post leadership, the same pattern kept repeating: creative teams move faster when the operational layer is structured correctly. The Framework captures that structure so editors and assistants do not have to reinvent it production by production.

PostOpz now extends that same operating logic into cloud editorial infrastructure, training, and AI-agent access—without separating the knowledge from the professional experience that created it.

Why I started PostOpz
Editors should spend their time shaping story—not fighting the system around them.

PostOpz exists to solve the operational layer of post production: the systems, support, structure, and workflow thinking that keep editorial moving. What started as a way to improve project organization and assistant-editor workflows evolved into a broader platform for training, cloud infrastructure, and agent-ready editorial intelligence.
