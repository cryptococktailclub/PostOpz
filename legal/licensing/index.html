  • Use supplied structures, checklists, and implementation guidance in the purchaser's own professional workflow, subject to any asset-specific instructions.
  • Quote or paste limited excerpts into an AI assistant for transient, user-directed clarification of the purchaser's own work, provided the excerpts are not retained or accumulated to create a persistent knowledge base.
